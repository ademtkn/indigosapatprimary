Our Programs

What makes Indigo Sapat different: the Singapore method in maths, a lot of English with native-speaking teachers, and four subjects that are part of every child’s timetable.

Special subjects

Lessons that set us apart

Unlike most private schools, these are regular lessons in the timetable for every class, not paid clubs after school.

English Maths In English

Children already study mathematics in Russian or Kyrgyz. English Maths is an additional lesson where they count, measure and solve problems in English.

  • Numbers, shapes and measurements in English
  • Word problems read and explained in English
  • Mathematical vocabulary used in international schools

The goal: more English practice, in a subject children already understand.

Robotics

In robotics lessons children work in teams, build models step by step and bring them to life with simple programming.

  • Building with construction kits
  • First steps in programming and algorithms
  • Teamwork, projects and presentations

Robotics trains engineering thinking, patience and creativity.

Chess

From the first moves of each piece to real games and school tournaments, chess is part of our timetable.

  • Rules, tactics and simple strategies
  • Games with classmates and school tournaments
  • Fair play, winning and losing with dignity

Chess develops concentration, memory, logic and self-control.

Magic Science In English

Colour changes, bubbles, magnets, volcanoes: children watch, try and explain experiments, and learn the science words in English.

  • Safe hands-on experiments in every lesson
  • Observing, predicting and explaining
  • Science vocabulary in English

Children fall in love with science and practise English at the same time.

Mathematics

The Singapore method

Our teachers teach mathematics with the Singapore method, used by schools around the world. Children move step by step from real objects to pictures and only then to numbers and symbols.

  1. Step 1 · Concrete

    Touch it

    Children work with real objects: cubes, counters, coins.

  2. Step 2 · Pictorial

    Draw it

    They show the problem as a picture or a bar model.

  3. 3 + 2 = 5

    Step 3 · Abstract

    Write it

    Only then do they write it with numbers and symbols.

The result: children understand the idea behind each operation and can explain how they solved a problem.

English focus

English every day

English has a special place at Indigo Sapat. Our aim is simple: children should hear and speak English as much as possible.

  • Native-speaking teachersOur English team includes teachers whose native language is English.
  • English lessonsSpeaking, listening, reading and writing in small, lively lessons.
  • English MathsAn extra maths lesson taught entirely in English.
  • Magic ScienceScience experiments explained in English.
