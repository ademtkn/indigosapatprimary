Academics

We follow the state curriculum of the Kyrgyz Republic for grades 1–6, teach mathematics with the Singapore method and add a strong English programme and four special subjects.

How we teach

Our approach to learning

Singapore method

Concrete → pictorial → abstract: children understand why, not only how.

Learning by doing

Building, experimenting and playing make knowledge last.

English in many lessons

English lessons plus English Maths and Magic Science taught in English.

Clear feedback

Regular assessment and open conversations with parents about progress.

Language of instruction

Russian-medium and Kyrgyz-medium classes

Families choose the language their child learns in. Both streams follow the same programme and have the same special subjects.

RU

Russian-medium classes

Main subjects are taught in Russian.

  • Russian language and literary reading
  • Mathematics (Singapore method)
  • Kyrgyz as a subject
KG

Kyrgyz-medium classes

Main subjects are taught in Kyrgyz.

  • Kyrgyz language and literary reading
  • Mathematics (Singapore method)
  • Russian as a subject

In both streams: English, English Maths, Robotics, Chess and Magic Science.

Grades 1–6

From first letters to middle school

Grades 1–2

First steps

A gentle start with a class teacher who knows each child well.

  • Reading, writing and numbers
  • Singapore maths with real objects
  • English songs, games and first words
  • Chess basics and first robots

Grades 3–4

Growing skills

More independence, bigger problems and longer projects.

  • Fluent reading and writing
  • Bar models and problem solving
  • English Maths and Magic Science in English
  • Robotics projects and chess games

Grades 5–6

Ready for more

Strong foundations and study habits for middle school.

  • Confident speaking in three languages
  • Logical thinking and research projects
  • Presentations in English
  • Chess and robotics competitions
