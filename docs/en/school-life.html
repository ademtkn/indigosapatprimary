School Life

School is more than lessons. It is friendship, games, celebrations and the feeling of belonging to a family.

A typical day

A day at Indigo Sapat

  1. 08:00

    Good morning!

    Teachers welcome children at the door.

  2. 08:30

    Lessons begin

    Core subjects while minds are fresh.

  3. 10:00

    Snack and play

    A break to eat, move and talk with friends.

  4. 12:30

    Lunch

    Time to eat together and rest.

  5. 13:30

    Robotics, chess, clubs

    Special lessons, clubs and supervised homework.

Times are indicative and may differ by grade. Please confirm with the school office.
