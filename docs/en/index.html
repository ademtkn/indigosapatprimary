Welcome

Where curious children love to learn

The first school years shape how a child feels about learning for the rest of their life. At Indigo Sapat Primary we want every boy and girl to leave in the morning happy to go to school and to come home with a new question.

Children study in Russian-medium or Kyrgyz-medium classes, learn mathematics with the Singapore method, and practise English every day, also in English Maths and Magic Science lessons. Robotics and chess are part of the timetable, not an extra.

Why Indigo Sapat

What makes our school different

Singapore maths

Our teachers use the Singapore method: children first touch, then draw, then write. Understanding comes before memorising.

English every day

English has a special place in our school, with native-speaking teachers on staff and extra lessons taught in English.

Subjects others don’t have

Robotics, chess, Magic Science (in English) and English Maths are regular lessons in the timetable.

Two language streams

Russian-medium and Kyrgyz-medium classes, for boys and girls together, in one friendly school family.
