Admissions

We are glad that you are considering Indigo Sapat for your child. We will guide you through every step.

Step by step

How to join us

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Write to us on Instagram or call the school office to ask about places in your child’s grade and stream.

  2. 2

    Visit the school

    Tour the building, see lessons and meet our teachers.

  3. 3

    Meet and get to know

    A friendly conversation with your child. From Grade 2, a short check of reading and maths.

  4. 4

    Submit documents

    Bring the documents from the list below to the school office.

  5. 5

    Welcome to Indigo Sapat

    Sign the agreement and get ready for the first day of school.

Documents

What to prepare

  • Copy of the birth certificate
  • Copies of both parents’ passports
  • Medical record (form 026/u) and vaccination card
  • Personal file and report card from the previous school (for transfers)
  • 3×4 cm photographs, 4 pieces
  • Application form completed at the school office

The final list may change. Please confirm it with the school office before your visit.

Tuition fees

Tuition is set for each academic year. Please contact the school office for current fees and payment options.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which grades do you teach?

Indigo Sapat Primary teaches grades 1 to 6. Boys and girls study together.

In which language are lessons taught?

We have Russian-medium and Kyrgyz-medium classes. You choose the stream when you apply. English, English Maths and Magic Science are taught in English in both streams.

Do you have native English speakers?

Yes. Our English team includes teachers whose native language is English.

What is the Singapore method?

A way of teaching maths in three steps: real objects, then pictures, then numbers and symbols. Children understand ideas deeply instead of memorising rules.

Are robotics and chess extra paid clubs?

No. Robotics, chess, Magic Science and English Maths are lessons in the regular timetable.

Can we visit before applying?

Yes, and we recommend it. Contact us to agree a convenient day and time.
