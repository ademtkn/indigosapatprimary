About Us

Indigo Sapat Primary is a primary school of the Indigo Academy network in Bishkek. We bring together strong teaching methods, a lot of English and the warm atmosphere of a family.

Our story

A primary school with a clear idea

Indigo Sapat Primary is part of Indigo Academy, a network of private schools in Bishkek. Our school is dedicated to the most important years: grades 1 to 6.

Our idea is simple: give children a strong foundation in reading and mathematics, let them use English every day, and teach them to think, through robotics, chess and science experiments. Boys and girls study together in Russian-medium and Kyrgyz-medium classes.

Our mission

To give every child a strong start: deep understanding, confident English and the joy of discovery.

Our vision

To be the primary school that families in Bishkek choose for a modern education rooted in good values.

Our values

Five words we live by

  1. CuriosityEvery question is a good question.
  2. Respectfor teachers, friends, parents and every person.
  3. EffortMistakes are part of learning; trying again is what counts.
  4. Kindnessshown in small actions every day.
  5. TeamworkWe build, play and learn together.

Director’s welcome

Dear parents

Thank you for your interest in Indigo Sapat Primary. Choosing the first school for your child is a big decision, and we are glad you are considering us.

In our classrooms children learn to understand, not only to remember. They solve problems with the Singapore method, speak English with native-speaking teachers, build robots and play chess. We want each child to feel noticed, supported and proud of their own progress.

I warmly invite you to visit us, to see our lessons and to talk with our teachers. Our doors are open.

Director, Indigo Sapat Primary
